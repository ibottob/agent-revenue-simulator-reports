🛠️ Methodology Notes

  • Algorithm: Simulated Annealing with adaptive cooling (acceptance-ratio feedback). Initial temp 10.0, cooling rate 0.95, min temp 0.01.
  • Search space: Fleet allocation (35 stations × counts), pricing (tariff selection, yield toggle, weekend factor, rate overrides, demand multipliers), marketing (segment campaigns × discount × max_customers).
  • Objective: Maximize mean net profit over stochastic seeds. No constraint penalties applied (all states satisfied hard constraints).
  • Stochastic evaluation: Each optimizer step evaluated with the same seed per run; multi-seed wrapper selects best across seeds.
  • Validation: Best config from seed 45 simulated once deterministically (seed=42) for final P&L figures.
  • Runtime environment: Python 3.11, local SQLite mock data (~3 MB), WSL2.
  • Known issue: The full-simulation P&L was identical across two different runs (3 seeds × 15 iters vs 5 seeds × 20 iters), suggesting convergence to a local optimum near baseline pricing.
