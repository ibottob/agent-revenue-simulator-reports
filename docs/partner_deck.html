The Problem

Fragmented Decisions

Isolated decisions in pricing, marketing, and fleet distribution prevent an optimal system-wide balance.

Pricing Blind Spots

High price surges may increase margin per ride but lead to a disproportionate drop in usage. Effectiveness varies significantly by location.

Marketing Inefficiency

Difficulty in identifying which customers need a coupon to book versus those who would pay full price anyway.

Static Allocation

Vehicles are often placed based on history, ignoring how current pricing or marketing measures are simultaneously shifting demand.

3 / 8
The Opportunity

Bridging the Gap

The Revenue Gap

Significant profit is lost when pricing, marketing, and vehicle locations are not synchronized to steer the right user to the right vehicle at the right time.

Holistic Steering

Moving from "optimal pricing" or "optimal allocation" to a system optimized for maximum total profit.

Our Solution

The Yield Optimization Engine

A unified model that predicts user behavior to propose and evaluate candidate strategies.

Input

Platform Action Space

Constraints on pricing steps, coupon values, and station capacity.

→
Searcher

Optimization Algorithm

Proposes smart combinations of pricing, marketing, and allocation.

→
Evaluator

Monte Carlo Simulator

Runs 10,000 probabilistic trials (365 days in 15-min steps) to account for human behavioral variance.

→
Output

The Manifests

Final actionable plans for Pricing, Allocation, and Marketing.

Output detail: Pricing (€ per km/min), Allocation (vehicle deployment), and Marketing (who gets what coupon and when).

The Foundation

User Digital Twins

⊕

Granular Prediction

We use rule-based and LLM-parameterized agents rather than broad user segments.

⊗

Maintaining Context

By modeling users individually, we preserve geographic information — a critical factor in booking behavior that is lost in traditional segmentation.

⊘

Probabilistic Logic

Each "Digital Twin" evaluates vehicle availability, price, and coupons to return a high-probability booking outcome (start time, end time, vehicle type, and distance).
